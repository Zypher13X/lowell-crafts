About

Lowell Crafts is a small crochet studio making pieces meant to be worn, used, and loved. Every item is hooked by hand — no machines, no shortcuts, no two exactly alike.

I started crocheting years ago as a way to slow down, and it stuck. Now I make everything from chunky beanies and cozy throws to tiny amigurumi and market totes. Natural fibers wherever possible, always soft against the skin.

Custom colorways and sizing are always welcome — just reach out on the contact page and we'll figure it out together.

How It's Made

Small batches. No shortcuts. Here's what goes into every piece.

  1. 01

    Choose the Yarn

    Every piece starts with picking the right fiber — softness, weight, and color all matter. I lean toward natural fibers: cotton, merino, and alpaca blends wherever possible.

  2. 02

    Swatch & Plan

    Before a single stitch goes into the final piece, I swatch to confirm tension and fit. Small batches mean I can adjust for every colorway rather than locking in a pattern and walking away.

  3. 03

    Hook by Hand

    Everything is worked by hand — no looms, no machines. This is where the time goes, and it's the part I love most. Each stitch is placed with intention.

  4. 04

    Finish & Block

    Blocking transforms a finished piece: stitches even out, edges relax, and the final shape emerges. It's the step most people skip. I never do.
